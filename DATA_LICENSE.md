# Farm frames — data licence and provenance

**Contents** — `artifacts/farm_frames_528.zip`: 528 JPEG frames (560 x 1312, 25 fps extraction) taken from
44 video clips, plus `farm_manifest_528.csv`.

**Provenance** — Recorded in a single acquisition session (May 2026) at a cattle farm in Jilin Province,
China, with a smartphone on a tripod at the feed bunk. The clips were recorded with the farm owner's
permission. Identity labels were assigned manually, one identity per clip, and were verified by the author.

**Licence** — Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

**You may** share and adapt the frames for non-commercial research, provided you attribute the source and
indicate changes. **You may not** use them commercially, and you may not attempt to re-identify the farm or
the animals beyond the identity labels supplied here.

**Known limitations to state in any reuse** — single session, one clip per identity; gallery and query frames
of an identity come from the same recording; 4 of the original 44 clips were byte-identical duplicates
(029=033, 030=034, 031=035, 032=036) and were treated as separate identities according to the author's
verified labelling; the frames are 560 px wide.
